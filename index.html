🤔 What Does It Mean By '2.5D' ?

Basically 2.5D games are the mixed form of 2D and 3D type visuals, This is good because it feels good even without using high storage and not too low storage as well.

Comparison Between 2D 🆚 3D :-

3D GAME

2D GAME

  • Use height, width, and depth (X, Y, and Z axes).

  • More realistic graphics but more complex to create.

  • Require more processing power and memory.

  • Examples: Minecraft, GTA V.
  • Use only height and width (X and Y axes).

  • Simpler graphics and easier to develop.

  • Usually require fewer system resources.

  • Examples: Super Mario Bros., Terraria.

2.5D Games

why '2.5d games' are better?

  • Visually Attractive – 2.5D games combine 2D gameplay with 3D graphics, creating a modern and appealing look.

  • Simple Gameplay – Players move mainly on a 2D plane, making the controls easy to learn and play.

  • Lower Development Cost – Compared to full 3D games, 2.5D games usually require less time, effort, and resources to develop.

  • Better Performance – They generally run more smoothly on low-end PCs and mobile devices because they are less demanding than fully 3D games.

  • Creative Level Design – Developers can use 3D environments, lighting, shadows, and camera effects while keeping the gameplay straightforward, resulting in an immersive experience.

The Game Assets

# Assets Used In Level 1-5 :-

1. Sprite Sheet - Mini Jinwoo
(Player Character)
  • This sprite sheet is of order 2×3 means 2 rows and 3 columns.
  • 1st row contains 3 frames of walk animation and 2nd row contains 2 frames of attack animation.
  • You will see this mini jinwoo till the level 5, after that you get a new upgraded jinwoo skins with new themes and battlefield.



2. Sprite Sheet - Goblin
(Enemy)
  • Order of sprite sheet:- 2×3.
  • 1st Row:- 3 frames of walk animation.
  • 2nd Row:- 3 frames of attack animation.
  • Goblin is the very weak enemy, only three strike and he will death.



3. The Dungeon
(battlefield)
  • The Battlefield is a 3d model background.
  • The file of this 3d background is of .gltf extension.
  • I get it for free from the website of 'Sketchfab'
click here to visit sketchfab website.

  • You will have to fight with this boss to move to level 6.
  • You will be rewarded a new Jinwoo skin after killing the white wolf boss!
