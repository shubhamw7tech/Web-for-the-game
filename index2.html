Details of Game Assets


Level 6-9 Game Assets:

Jinwoo Skin 2
(player character)
  • Order of Sprite sheet: 3×3.
  • 1st Row: Idle position (no movement).
  • 2nd Row: Motion State.
  • 3rd Row: Attacking State.
  • This jinwoo skin has a ability: it attacks in all direction even if its head is not toward that direction.



Orc
(Enemy)
  • Order of Sprite sheet: 3×3.
  • 1st Row: Idle position (no movement).
  • 2nd Row: Motion State.
  • 3rd Row: Attacking State.
  • In levels from 6-9, Orc will appear and duo orcs come at a same time to attack Jinwoo.



The Dark Night Purple Gate City
(battlefield)
  • This City background made with only codes.
  • The Purple Gate gives The Solo Leveling vibes.
  • The Aura effects matches the darkness.
  • This is Less glitchy then A Model background.
  • No Files needed, just the combination of HTML, CSS and JS.

Level 10 Game Assets:

Jinwoo skin 3
(only in Level 10)
  • Order of Sprite sheet: 3×3.
  • Mechanism of this skin is all same as above sprite sheets.
  • This time jinwoo throw Azure Nova(*blue aura light attacks) to fight with level 10 boss.
  • This skin is only for level 10, after that you will get a new skin again in further levels.



Azure Nova
(jinwoo's throw attack)
  • Its a png file.
  • This look like plasma trail glowing effect.
  • Jinwoo is not perfectly able to stay close to Level 10 Boss, so this attack give him advantage.



Giant Dragon Snake
(Level 10 boss)
  • Its a 3D model(*with .gltf extension).
  • Giant Snake dragon boss revolve around the jinwoo.
  • This boss keep falling poisonous potions from the air to jinwoo, its damage is highly effective.
  • If the boss health down when you damage him a lot, then the no of potions falling on jinwoo increases, So make sure you dodge them perfectly.
